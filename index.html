Why Investors Choose Grafton

Built for the investor, not the homeowner

Unlike traditional mortgage lenders that focus on owner-occupied properties, Grafton works exclusively with real estate investors seeking scalable financing. Our mission is to structure financing that aligns with your strategy rather than forcing your strategy to fit rigid guidelines.

Fast underwriting decisions

Fix and flip and bridge can close in 3 to 5 days in most states, with no appraisal needed in most of them.

Investor-focused underwriting

We underwrite project viability, property value and your strategy. No W-2 documentation and no traditional income verification.

Flexible DSCR structures

15, 20, 30 and 40-year terms, interest-only options, prepayment periods from 0 to 5 years, single property or portfolio.

Rental, STR and build-to-rent

Experience across long-term rental, short-term rental and build-to-rent assets, so the exit is part of the first conversation.

A hybrid model, stated plainly

Grafton is a direct lender on select bridge and fix and flip programs, and a nationwide loan broker across DSCR and rental products. That means the capital behind your loan depends on the program, and we will tell you which one you are in rather than leaving you to work it out at closing.

How It Works

From pricing to funding

Step One

Get loan pricing

Tell us about the property and the project through the pricing questionnaire. Six questions, and it goes to the team rather than a general inbox. You are not asked for a document package before anyone has looked at the deal.
Step Two

Underwriting built around the asset

We underwrite project viability, property value and your strategy. On fix and flip and bridge that means after-repair value rather than employment income. On rental it means the property's cash flow, with a DSCR as low as 1.0.
Step Three

Direct or placed, and we say which

Grafton is a direct lender on select bridge and fix and flip programs and a nationwide broker across DSCR and rental products. You will know which side of that your loan sits on.
Step Four

Closing and funding

Fix and flip and bridge can close in 3 to 5 days in most states, with no appraisal needed in most of them. From initial pricing through to closing and funding, the same team stays on the file.
